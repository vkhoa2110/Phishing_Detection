The CSV/JSONL prediction artifacts derive URLs, IDs, source labels and confidence
tiers from this dataset. The evaluation adds model scores, LLM responses and
metrics. It retains the original split and label meanings. Credit the source
when redistributing dataset-derived rows. Dataset URLs are research inputs;
the evaluation does not visit their destination pages.
